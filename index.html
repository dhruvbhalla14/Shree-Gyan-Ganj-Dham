The Divine Mother

Maa Laxmi Bala Tripur Sundari

Bala Tripur Sundari is the youthful, gentle form of the Divine Mother, a giver of knowledge, prosperity and peace. Devotees come to her with their questions, their gratitude and their new beginnings.

  • GyanWisdom and clarity for students, seekers and families.
  • LaxmiProsperity earned with dharma and shared with others.
  • SevaAnna daan, aarti and upkeep carried by the community.

Upcoming Celebrations

Festivals at the Dham

Join the lamps, the bhajans and the community meals. Dates follow the Panchang; the temple confirms muhurat timings closer to each day.

Fri, 6 November 2026

Dhanteras

Laxmi and Kuber puja in the evening. Devotees bring new utensils and coins for blessing.

Sun, 8 November 2026

Deepawali Maha Laxmi Puja

The grandest night of the year for Maa Laxmi. Thousands of diyas, a special shringar and the midnight aarti.

Fri, 20 November 2026

Dev Uthani Ekadashi

The awakening of the Lord after the holy months. Bhajan sandhya and Tulsi vivah celebrations.

Tue, 24 November 2026

Kartik Purnima

Dev Deepawali. Deep daan at dusk, followed by a full-moon Aarti.

Sat, 6 March 2027

Maha Shivaratri

Night-long abhishek and jagran at the Shiva shrine in the temple courtyard.
